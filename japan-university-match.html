Good to know

Read this before you shortlist

🎯Only 40 universities

Limited to schools on the JSPS Bangkok Office directory — a strong, verified starting point, but not the full picture of every Japanese university.

🏙️Living cost = city average

All 7 Tokyo schools in Hyper-Urban Core show the same $1,698/month — real costs vary by neighborhood.

🎓NAIST is grad-school only

It sits in the Golden Route group but has no undergraduate programs — relevant for master's/PhD applicants only.

💸Tuition discounts vary by school

Discount availability doesn't follow the archetype pattern — always confirm directly with each university.

📍One campus per university

Schools with multiple campuses (like Tokai University) are represented by their main campus only.

🧭A starting point, not the final word

Use this to narrow 40 options to a handful — then dig into the specific schools that match your vibe.

📅Data is from 2025 — check scores before applying

All admission scores, living costs, research figures, and program availability in this tool reflect data collected in 2025. The university archetypes (clusters) are based on structural patterns that are likely to remain stable over time — but admission selectivity scores can shift year by year due to applicant demand and university policy changes. Always verify the current admission requirements directly with each university before applying.

A note from the research team

We built this for you — and we hope it helps. 🇹🇭→🇯🇵

This tool was created by the Human-Centered AI Laboratory (KU-HCAI) at Kasetsart University as part of a peer-reviewed study published in IJIET. Our goal was simple: to make the process of choosing a Japanese university less overwhelming for Thai students by turning raw data into something you can actually use.

We genuinely hope this serves as a concrete, data-driven starting point for your decision — and that it opens doors to more research expanding beyond these 40 universities, across more countries, and with richer data sources year by year. Consider this a foundation, not a ceiling.
